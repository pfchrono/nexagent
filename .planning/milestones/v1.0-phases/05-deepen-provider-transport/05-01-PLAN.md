<objective>
Make provider transport trustworthy enough to use without letting transport semantics redefine product identity.
</objective>

<context>

Keep transport subordinate to shared runtime and harness behavior: surface configured provider truthfully, report failures explicitly, do not silently switch providers, and do not widen routing semantics without spec coverage.
</context>
